# VideoReferee — the solo spike

**This branch is history. The app it grew into lives at
[MartinFrancu/VRL](https://github.com/MartinFrancu/VRL), and runs at
[martinfrancu.github.io/VRL](https://martinfrancu.github.io/VRL/).**

Do not build from here. What is in `web/solo/` is a snapshot from partway
through: four ways of looking at a marked moment, a BOOKMARK button and a
slider. Everything after that — three modes instead of four, tapping the
picture to mark, dragging the picture to scrub, the orientation fix, the icon,
the name, the help screen — happened in the other repository.

## What it was for

One referee, one phone, no setup, as against the multi-camera VideoReferee on
the branches beside this one: several phones, a laptop on the venue's Wi-Fi,
and every angle of the same instant side by side. That one is worth its setup
when more than one angle matters. This was for when setting four phones up was
never going to happen.

It moved out because it needed to be public to be hosted, and because the two
share almost nothing: everything difficult in the multi-camera version exists
to reconcile several devices, and none of it applies to one.
